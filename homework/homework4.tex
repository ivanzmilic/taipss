{\bf Problem 1:} Show that, for a blackbody radiation, radiation pressure is equal to: 
\begin{equation}
p_{\rm rad} = \frac{u}{3} = \frac{4 \sigma}{3c} T^4.
\end{equation}

\emph{Hint: } One of the ways to solve this is to consider that the radiation is impinging on a perfectly absorbing wall and that the intensity of the radiation is given by the Planck function (recall the first homework). Then relate the intensity of the radiation to the number of photons, and that number of photons to the momentum the photonos are transferring to the wall.
